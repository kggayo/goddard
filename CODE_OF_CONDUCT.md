# Be a good human to the humans

Goddard welcomes contributors of every experience level and background. This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces.

- Be patient with questions and first contributions.
- Critique the code or idea, not the person.
- Explain disagreements clearly and leave room to change your mind.
- Respect boundaries, privacy, identities, and different perspectives.
- Give credit and avoid presenting other people's work as your own.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not welcome. Don't publish someone's private information or credentials.

## When something goes wrong

Use GitHub's **Report content / Report abuse** controls on the relevant content to request private platform review. For a public project-moderation request, tag `@kggayo` only if you are comfortable discussing it publicly; don't include sensitive personal details. You are not expected to resolve harassment through an argument in a thread.

The maintainer may clarify expectations, edit or remove inappropriate content, lock a discussion, or restrict participation. Repeated or serious misconduct can result in a ban. Reports and moderation should be handled respectfully and with as little disclosure of personal information as possible.

Project moderation is currently led by [@kggayo](https://github.com/kggayo). There is no promised response time; GitHub's reporting tools remain available for urgent platform-safety concerns.
